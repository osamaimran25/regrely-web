## Long description

RegRely is a privacy and compliance operations platform built for growing
companies that need serious GDPR workflows without the cost and implementation
weight of an enterprise privacy suite.

The platform gives teams one operational record for recurring privacy work:

- DSAR intake, identity checks, deadlines, review and response evidence
- ROPA and data-processing records with purpose, lawful basis, retention,
  recipients, vendors and accountable owners
- Consent status, notice versions and withdrawal history
- GDPR compliance assessments, findings, owners and remediation evidence
- Privacy and compliance risk scoring, mitigation and residual-risk acceptance
- Management reports and audit-ready evidence exports

RegRely is suitable for founders, operations teams, privacy owners, legal
operations, security and GRC teams in SaaS, fintech, healthtech, ecommerce,
insurtech, edtech, logistics and HR. GDPR workflows are available today.
CCPA, HIPAA and UAE PDPL support are described as roadmap items and must not be
represented as currently supported.
