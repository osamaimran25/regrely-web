## Industry pages

Each industry page targets the pattern `[industry] + privacy/GDPR/compliance software`
and supports it with the data types, buyer concerns, and workflows specific to that
industry.

- SaaS: GDPR compliance software for SaaS, DPA, subprocessors, security reviews
- Healthtech: healthcare privacy software, HIPAA compliance management
- Fintech: fintech compliance software, privacy risk and regulatory evidence
- Ecommerce: ecommerce GDPR compliance, consent and customer data requests
- Insurtech: insurance privacy and compliance, policyholder and claims data
- Edtech: student data privacy software, guardian consent and retention
- Logistics: logistics data privacy, cross-border transfers and vendor risk
- HR: employee DSAR software, candidate and workforce data privacy

## Informational clusters already published

- Regulations: GDPR vs CCPA, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, HIPAA, UAE PDPL

## Measurement

Track by landing page and query cluster:

- Search impressions and non-branded clicks
- Average position and click-through rate
- Trial starts, demo requests, and assisted conversions
- Indexed canonical URLs and crawl errors
- Backlinks and referring domains to guide/pillar pages

Technical and on-page work creates eligibility, not guaranteed rankings. Search Console
submission, credible backlinks, expert review, regular updates, and real customer proof
remain the principal off-site and trust-building work.
